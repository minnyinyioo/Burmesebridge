# BurmeseBridge HSK Vocabulary Artwork Standard

This file is the persistent production standard for every HSK vocabulary illustration.

- Produce original square artwork at a consistent source size and display it with `object-fit: contain`.
- Keep every important subject inside the central 80% safe area with generous margins. Never crop heads, hands, feet, logos, or the teaching object.
- Use BurmeseBridge IP V2: the recurring friendly young Asian learning guide with the same wavy black hair, facial proportions, skin tone, and polished editorial-anime line work.
- Lock the palette: jacket `#0E5A49`, warm ivory background `#FFF8ED`, cream clothing, restrained terracotta accents, and soft natural light. Do not drift toward blue-green, neon green, yellow-green, or heavily saturated skin tones.
- A crisp white-outlined green `B` brand mark on the character's outer clothing is mandatory and must remain visible. The green crossbody bag is optional: include it only when natural for the scene, and omit it when it obstructs the action or teaching object. If a bag is present, it should also carry the `B` mark.
- Communicate one vocabulary meaning clearly through a believable, lively action and scene. Props, body language, location, time, and relationships must support the exact word rather than merely decorate the image. Do not place Chinese, Burmese, English captions, or unreliable generated text inside the artwork.
- Keep Burmese definitions and accessible Burmese alt text in `lib/hskVocabularyMy.ts`.
- Generate and review assets in batches of 20. Confirm semantic accuracy first, then clothing-logo presence, palette compliance, scene logic, safe-area composition, consistent character identity, and file integrity before deployment. Never infer filename-to-word mapping from generation order; inspect each final image before binding it to vocabulary data.
- Store final project assets under `public/images/hsk/vocabulary/` and use stable names beginning with `hsk<level>-`.
- Generated artwork is BurmeseBridge-owned project material; keep source provenance and do not use copyrighted third-party characters or artwork.

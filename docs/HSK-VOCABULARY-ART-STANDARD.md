# BurmeseBridge HSK Vocabulary Artwork Standard

This file is the persistent production standard for every HSK vocabulary illustration.

- Produce original square artwork at a consistent source size and display it with `object-fit: contain`.
- Keep every important subject inside the central 80% safe area with generous margins. Never crop heads, hands, feet, logos, or the teaching object.
- Use BurmeseBridge IP V2: the recurring friendly young Asian learning guide, dark BurmeseBridge green jacket, cream clothing, green sneakers, and green crossbody bag.
- A clean white-outlined green `B` brand mark must be visibly integrated on both the jacket chest and the bag. Do not add third-party marks.
- Use warm ivory backgrounds, forest green and muted terracotta accents, soft natural light, and a polished editorial anime illustration style.
- Communicate one vocabulary meaning clearly through the scene. Do not place Chinese, Burmese, English captions, or unreliable generated text inside the artwork.
- Keep Burmese definitions and accessible Burmese alt text in `lib/hskVocabularyMy.ts`.
- Generate and review assets in batches of 20. Confirm semantic accuracy, logo presence, safe-area composition, consistent character identity, and file integrity before deployment.
- Store final project assets under `public/images/hsk/vocabulary/` and use stable names beginning with `hsk<level>-`.
- Generated artwork is BurmeseBridge-owned project material; keep source provenance and do not use copyrighted third-party characters or artwork.
